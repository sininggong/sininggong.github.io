I want students to leave my courses able to connect mathematical ideas, explain why an argument works, and judge its correctness independently. My teaching connects larger mathematical questions with careful work on definitions, examples, and proofs.

## Teaching Approach

I prepare materials around clear mathematical goals and use examples, graphs, and computation to connect ideas. Students practice explaining arguments, checking assumptions, and identifying gaps in reasoning. In graduate recitations, students present mathematical material and reflect on their explanations. These activities also help me identify what needs more attention.

Patient listening and constructive feedback are central to my teaching. I use student questions, anonymous feedback, and annotated solutions to clarify expectations and adapt my materials. My psychology background informs my attention to motivation and students' different starting points. In the AI era, I emphasize the mathematical judgment needed to evaluate an argument independently.
